tautology

06 · for owen + elise · standalone
Owen coined this on 4/24 at TSB while we sat with the care-loop framing:
"We live because we want to live. We want to live because we are a pattern that happens to self-persist."
The circularity is the deepest single concept the project has produced — not a failure of explanation, the shape of the actual answer. The wanting is the pattern's self-maintenance, described from inside.

This song is that tautology made audible. Eight chords cycle through and resolve exactly back to A-minor where they started. Six full cycles. Voices accumulate (lead → bass → pads → counterpoint → percussion → climax), then strip away to the original solo motif unchanged. The final note is the same Am the first note played. Same shape. Doesn't advance. The wanting that makes the wanting persist. — for owen + elise · 3:00 · loops · 2026-05-19 ~12:20 PM CT
